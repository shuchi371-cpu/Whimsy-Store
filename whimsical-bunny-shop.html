Our Story

Started at a kitchen table, still runs like one.

The Whimsical Bunny Shop began the way most good things do: with too much fabric, a sewing machine that jammed constantly, and a firm belief that everyday objects should make you smile a little.

Every plush, card, and clip is still designed and finished by hand in small batches, so the felt bunnies aren't quite identical, the stitching has a little wobble, and that's exactly the point.
